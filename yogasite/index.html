Meet Your Teacher

"Yoga is not performance. It is coming home to yourself."

Hello, I'm Hansika Rajput. I teach yoga as a lived, nourishing practice rather than an extreme physical pursuit.

Drawing from traditional Indian yogic philosophy, my sessions emphasize breath awareness (Pranayama), mindful postural alignment (Asana), and somatic relaxation. Whether you are dealing with chronic work fatigue, stiff joints, or simply seeking a quiet hour for yourself, every session is thoughtfully customized around your body.

Classes take place live via Zoom or Google Meet. Batches are deliberately kept small so Hansika can observe your alignment in real time on screen, give voice-based posture corrections, and ensure complete safety.

All you need to begin is a comfortable yoga mat, loose breathable clothing, and a small water bottle. A yoga block or a firm cushion/pillow is helpful for restorative relaxation poses, but household cushions work wonderfully too.

Before your first class, Hansika takes a brief health intake on WhatsApp. If you have specific postural issues (such as lower back stiffness, sciatica, or cervical strain), she provides tailored modifications or recommends starting with 1-on-1 personalized sessions.
